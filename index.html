5-Day Gym Plan

Day 1: Back and Biceps

1. Lat Pulldown

Sit on the lat pulldown machine with knees under pads. Grab the bar with a wide grip. Pull down to upper chest, squeezing back muscles, then return slowly. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

2. Seated Cable Row

Sit at the cable row machine, feet on platform. Grab handle, keep back straight, pull towards abdomen, squeezing back, then return. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

3. One-Arm Dumbbell Row

Place left knee and hand on bench. Hold dumbbell in right hand, pull to side, elbow close, then lower. Switch sides. 3 sets of 10-12 reps per side.

4. Inverted Row

Lie under a bar, grab with overhand grip, body straight. Pull chest to bar, lower back. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

5. Dumbbell Curl

Stand, hold dumbbells with palms forward. Curl weights to shoulders, lower slowly. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

6. Hammer Curl

Stand, hold dumbbells with palms facing in. Curl weights to shoulders, lower slowly. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

7. Face Pull

Stand at cable machine, rope at face level, pull to face, elbows out, return. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.

Day 2: Chest and Triceps

1. Bench Press

Lie on bench, hold barbell with wide grip, lower to chest, press up. Use light weights. 3 sets of 8-10 reps.

2. Dumbbell Flye

Lie on bench, hold dumbbells above chest, lower out to sides, bring back up. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

3. Push-Up

Hands on floor, body straight, lower chest to ground, push up. Modify with knees. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.

4. Incline Dumbbell Press

Lie on incline bench, hold dumbbells above chest, lower to chest, press up. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

5. Tricep Pushdown

Stand at cable machine, grab bar, push down until arms straight, return slowly. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

6. Overhead Dumbbell Extension

Stand or sit, hold dumbbell above head, lower behind head, extend arms. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

Day 2 Complete!

Day 3: Legs

1. Leg Press

Sit in leg press machine, feet on platform, push weight up, lower slowly. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

2. Leg Extension

Sit on leg extension machine, shins under pad, extend legs, lower back. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.

3. Leg Curl

Lie face down on leg curl machine, heels under pad, curl up, lower back. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

4. Step-Up

Stand in front of bench, step up with one foot, stand, step down. Alternate. 3 sets of 10-12 reps per leg.

5. Bodyweight Squat

Stand, feet shoulder-width, lower until thighs parallel, stand up. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.

6. Goblet Squat

Hold dumbbell close to chest, feet shoulder-width, squat until thighs parallel, stand up. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

Day 3 Complete!

Day 4: Shoulders and Abs

1. Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Sit or stand, hold dumbbells at shoulder height, press overhead, lower back. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

2. Lateral Raise

Stand, hold dumbbells at sides, raise to shoulder height, lower back. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.

3. Front Raise

Stand, hold dumbbells in front, raise to shoulder height, lower back. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.

4. Crunch

Lie on back, knees bent, hands behind head, lift shoulders, lower back. 3 sets of 15-20 reps.

5. Plank

Rest on forearms and toes, keep body straight, hold for 30-60 seconds. 3 sets of 30-60 seconds.

6. Bicycle Crunch

Lie on back, hands behind head, elbow to opposite knee, switch sides. 3 sets of 15-20 reps per side.

7. Russian Twist

Sit, lean back slightly, lift feet, twist torso side to side. 3 sets of 20 reps per side.

Day 4 Complete!

Day 5: Full Body Light

1. Bodyweight Squat

Stand, feet shoulder-width, lower until thighs parallel, stand up. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.

2. Push-Up

Hands on floor, body straight, lower chest, push up. Modify with knees. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.

3. Incline Dumbbell Press

Lie on incline bench, hold dumbbells above chest, lower to chest, press up. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

4. Face Pull

Stand at cable machine, rope at face level, pull to face, elbows out, return. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.

5. Dumbbell Curl

Stand, hold dumbbells with palms forward. Curl weights to shoulders, lower slowly. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

6. Tricep Pushdown

Stand at cable machine, grab bar, push down until arms straight, return slowly. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.

7. Plank

Rest on forearms and toes, keep body straight, hold for 30-60 seconds. 3 sets of 30-60 seconds.
